<objective>
Centralise repeated test scaffolding into reusable fixtures, refactor existing tests to consume them, and delete two confirmed-orphan frontend components. Closes REQ-test-fixture-dedup.

Purpose: Test files currently re-construct `Flight(...)` inline (7+ call sites in `test_flight_models.py`) AND `FlightQuery(...)` inline (10+ call sites in `test_flight_models.py`), and inline-parse SSE responses (`test_session.py` lines 60-63).
